title: "Tenant Isolation and the Platform Role"
description: "How FrankPHP keeps each tenant's data separate, what the platform role can do across tenants, and the rules that keep it safe."
---

FrankPHP keeps each tenant's data separate, at two levels:

- **On every request,** the tenant in the address must match the signed-in person's tenant. AuthMiddleware turns anyone else away. See [Middleware](/concepts/middleware).
- **On every database query,** the inherited model methods — `find()`, `all()`, `where()`, `delete()` — need a tenant, and only ever touch that tenant's rows.

---

## What the platform role can do across tenants

The `platform` role is for the people who run the site. In the starter app, it's the only role that reaches beyond its own tenant:

| Power | Where |
|---|---|
| **See every tenant's users** | The Users screen, with a Tenant column |
| **Edit or delete any tenant and its users** | **Edit User** and **Edit Tenant**, opened from the Users screen |
| **Sign in as a tenant's owner,** to help them | **Capture Tenant** on Edit Tenant. While it's on, the top bar turns red and shows *Acting as Tenant Owner*; **Release Tenant** switches back. |

---

## The rules that keep it safe

<Steps>
  <Step title="Only the signed-in person's role decides">
    Whether someone gets platform powers depends only on the role of the person signed in, read on the server. Nothing in an address, query string or form can switch them on for anyone else.
  </Step>
  <Step title="It still works one tenant at a time">
    Even the all-tenants list is built tenant by tenant, using the same tenant-locked model methods. FrankPHP never runs a query across every tenant at once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Every check happens on the server">
    Someone with the `user` role who visits `/tenant/{id}/users` is sent back to their dashboard with `?error=forbidden`. Hiding the menu links is only a convenience. If you change your navigation, never rely on a hidden link for security.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Signing in as an owner is guarded">
    Capture Tenant and Release Tenant are form posts carrying the security token, and each gives the session a new id. Before switching back, FrankPHP checks the person is still a platform user — if not, their session ends.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
**Treat `platform` as a highly privileged role.** Anyone with it can see the email, name, role and login activity of every user, change or delete any tenant, and sign in as any tenant's owner. **No record is kept** of signing in as an owner. Give the role only to the people who run the site.
</Warning>

<Note>
The framework supplies the operations and the sign-in-as-owner routes. The Users, Edit User and Edit Tenant screens belong to the starter app, so who sees them is your app's decision. See [Revisions and Migrations](/concepts/revisions-and-migrations).
</Note>
