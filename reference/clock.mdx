`Frank\Core\Clock` gives every FrankPHP app the same rule for dates and times:

> **UTC at rest. Local at the edges.**

Store exact moments in UTC. Read what people type in their own timezone. Show dates in their timezone. Work out date filters in PHP, then send UTC to the database.

```php
use Frank\Core\Clock;
```

`Clock` is a set of static methods. It needs no setup and no container recipe.

---

## What Clock does — and doesn't

`Clock` does the mechanical work: the current UTC time, offsets such as `+1 hour`, checking and choosing timezones, converting between local time and UTC, and the UTC boundaries of a local date.

It doesn't decide what anything **means**. Labels such as *Today*, *Never* or *Overdue* are your app's decisions, so they belong in your services.

---

## Which timezone

Most date code starts by choosing the timezone:

```php
$timezone = Clock::resolveTimezone($request->user, $request->tenant, $config);
```

It takes the first valid one of these:

<Steps>
  <Step title="The user's timezone">
    From the user's own settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The tenant's timezone">
    If the user hasn't set one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The app's timezone">
    `APP_TIMEZONE`, from `app/.env`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="UTC">
    If none of the above is set, or valid.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Always pass the user, tenant and settings in. `Clock` never reads the session, the request or the database for itself.
</Note>

---

## Where to use it

| Where | Use Clock? | Why |
|---|:---:|---|
| Service | Yes | The best place: conversions, labels and date filters |
| Controller | Sometimes | Fine for converting what was posted, or simple steps |
| Model | No | Models are given UTC values by the service, and never call `Clock` |
| View | No | Views are given values ready to show |
| SQL | No | SQL is given UTC values, and never works out local dates |

<Warning>
- Don't use `date()`, `time()` or `new DateTime('now')` for anything you save.
- Don't use SQL's `NOW()` or `CURRENT_TIMESTAMP` for your app's timestamps.
- Don't call `Clock` from a view.
</Warning>
