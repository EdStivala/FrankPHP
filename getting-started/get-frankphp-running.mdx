title: "Get FrankPHP Running"
description: "Install FrankPHP on shared hosting, configure it with a standard .env file, and sign in. Eight simple steps, usually under 15 minutes."
---

By the end of this guide you'll have FrankPHP installed on a subdomain, configured with a standard `.env` file, and be signed in as a real user. That's enough to know the framework is working and to get a feel for how it thinks.

There are eight simple steps, and it usually takes less than 15 minutes to get the framework installed, set up and fully running.

Your host needs **PHP 8.5+**, **MySQL or MariaDB**, and an email account FrankPHP can send from (SMTP). Nothing else. No Composer, no Node, no build tools.

1. [Create your MySQL database](/getting-started/step-1)
2. [Configure FrankPHP with `.env`](/getting-started/step-2)
3. [Set up your server folders](/getting-started/step-3)
4. [Upload FrankPHP](/getting-started/step-4)
5. [Point your subdomain](/getting-started/step-5)
6. [Let FrankPHP initialise the database](/getting-started/step-6)
7. [Open FrankPHP in your browser](/getting-started/step-7)
8. [Sign in](/getting-started/step-8)
