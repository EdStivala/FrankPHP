Nothing to do here by hand. The first time you visit the site, FrankPHP checks whether its core tables exist and, if not, creates them automatically from `framework/sql/schema.core.sql`.

A fresh install starts empty: there are no starter tenants and no starter accounts. You create the first one yourself when you sign in, in Step 8.

<Note>
If you'd rather review the schema before your first visit, you can still import `framework/sql/schema.core.sql` manually through phpMyAdmin's **Import** tab, or by pasting its contents into the **SQL** command window. This is entirely optional — FrankPHP creates it automatically either way.
</Note>
