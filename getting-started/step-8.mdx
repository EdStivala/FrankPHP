1. Enter your email address and select **Email me a code**.
2. FrankPHP emails you a 6-digit code. It lasts 15 minutes.
3. Type the code on the **Check your email** screen and select **Continue**.

Because your address has no account yet, FrankPHP creates a new tenant with you as its **owner**, and signs you in. You'll land on the default dashboard at:

```
/tenant/1/dashboard
```

That's the whole sign-up: there is no separate sign-up page and no password to set. You stay signed in for 28 days, renewed while you keep using the app.

<Note>
**No code arriving?** Check your spam folder, then your mail settings in `app/.env` — see [Getting Email Up and Running](/getting-started/getting-email-up-and-running).
</Note>

<Note>
Anyone else who signs in at `/login` with a new address gets **their own new tenant** and becomes its owner. See [Accessing The Site](/concepts/users/signup-flow).
</Note>
