---
title: "Accessing The Site"
description: "How someone new gets into your app: one screen, an emailed code, and their own tenant with them as its owner."
---

FrankPHP has one screen for signing in and signing up, at `/login`. There are no passwords. Everyone proves their email address with a 6-digit code — and if the address is new, FrankPHP creates their account and their own tenant there and then.

---

## The steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter an email address">
    At `/login` the person types their email address and selects **Email me a code**. The screen looks the same whether or not the address already has an account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get the code">
    FrankPHP emails a **6-digit code**, valid for **15 minutes**. The email holds the code only, never a link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type the code">
    On the **Check your email** screen they type the code and select **Continue**. Three wrong guesses cancel the code. **Didn't receive a code? Send a new one** sends a fresh one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Agree to the terms (if you've turned them on)">
    Only for a new address, and only if your app has turned on [Terms & Conditions acceptance](/concepts/users/terms-acceptance). The **One last step** screen asks them to tick *I have read and agree to the Terms & Conditions* and select **Continue**. Nothing is created until they do.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Account created">
    For a new address, FrankPHP creates a new tenant, creates the person as its **owner**, and signs them in — with no "create an account?" question. For an address that already has an account, it simply signs them in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Dashboard">
    They land on their tenant's dashboard, at `/tenant/{tenant_id}/dashboard`, and stay signed in for 28 days as long as they keep using the app.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
**Continue with Google** works the same way, if you've turned it on. Google confirms the email address instead of a code, then steps 4 to 6 follow as above. See [Environment Configuration](/architecture/environment-configuration#google-and-microsoft-sign-in).
</Note>

---

## Good to know

<Tip>
- **No email probing.** Known and new addresses behave exactly the same until the code is right, so nobody can use the screen to find out who has an account.
- **One email, one account.** An email address can belong to only one user, in one tenant.
- **Rate limiting.** At most 5 codes can be requested per hour from one IP address.
- **All or nothing.** The tenant and the user are created together. If anything fails, neither is created and the person sees *"Account creation failed. Please try again."*
- **Site-owner alerts.** The address in `MAIL_SITE_ADMIN` gets an email when an address with no account asks for a code, and another when the account is created. See [Getting Email Up and Running](/getting-started/getting-email-up-and-running).
- **First login recorded.** Signing in straight after sign-up counts as the person's first login, so they don't show as "never logged in" on the [User Screen](/concepts/users/users-dashboard).
- **Invited people skip all this.** Someone invited by email follows the link to a welcome screen, and their account is created when they select **Let me see**. No code is needed.
</Tip>
